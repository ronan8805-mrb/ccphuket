About Us

California quality. Island roots. Officially licensed in Thailand.

from cali to the cave

Cali Cave began with a simple idea: bring the highest California cannabis standards to Thailand’s most beautiful islands — fully licensed, fully regulated, and curated for people who know the difference. We built a club that feels premium, trustworthy, and unmistakably Cali — without leaving Thai hospitality behind.

Origin

Born from California quality standards and rooted in the islands. Cali Cave is Thailand’s premier licensed cannabis club — designed for travelers and locals who expect the real thing: top-shelf flower, concentrates, and exclusive drops from the world’s best brands.

Licensed & Regulated

We operate as an officially licensed and regulated cannabis club in Thailand. Compliance is non-negotiable. Every product we stock meets our standards for quality, transparency, and legal retail. Guests 20+ are welcome at our Phuket flagship.

Mission

We don’t just stock shelves — we curate experiences. From Wizard Trees and Doja Pak to John Doe, SuperDope, and Exotics 710, we bring California and international excellence to Phuket now, and Koh Samui from 31st October 2026.
